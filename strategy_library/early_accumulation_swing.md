# early_accumulation_swing

## Philosophy

- Find names that are still building quietly before the broader market clearly recognizes the move.
- This is our preferred “before the run” setup.

## Setup Rules

- Price is still in a manageable area of the 20-day and 60-day ranges.
- Support is relatively close.
- RSI is constructive but not overbought.
- MACD is turning or already modestly positive.
- Volume is stable to improving.
- Fundamentals are not breaking down and ideally are improving.
- Sector context should be neutral-to-improving, not collapsing.

## Disqualifiers

- Already extended chart.
- Price too close to the short-term high.
- Support drifted too far away.
- Weak primary-data freshness or major structural risk.

## Outcome Tracking

- 1-day: does the base remain intact?
- 3-day: is there accumulation follow-through?
- 5-day: does the name move from base to watchable run?
- 10-day: did the early read catch a real move or only noise?
- 20-day: did the early accumulation read turn into a meaningful swing advance?

## Analysis Template

- Distance from local lows: should be relatively close.
- Fundamental improvement: important.
- Volume confirmation: should be improving.
- Sector wake-up: important.
- Clear support: important.
- Dilution / structural risk: low.
