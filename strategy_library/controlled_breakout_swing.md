# controlled_breakout_swing

## Philosophy

- Catch a breakout early enough to matter, but only when the move still looks controlled and not overheated.
- This is not a chase strategy. It is a selective breakout-confirmation strategy.

## Setup Rules

- Price presses a clear resistance area or breaks above it after a defined range.
- The prior range is visible enough to estimate a reasonable target and invalidation.
- RSI shows constructive momentum without extreme overheating.
- MACD is positive or turning positive.
- Volume should be at or above the 20-day average.
- ATR should stay manageable for swing holding risk.
- Fundamental quality should not contradict the chart.

## Disqualifiers

- Breakout already too stretched relative to the recent range.
- Price is too high in the 60-day range for a fresh entry.
- Weak volume on the breakout attempt.
- Structural red flags such as rights, units, warrants, or severe instability.

## Outcome Tracking

- 1-day: did price hold above the breakout zone?
- 3-day: did follow-through appear?
- 5-day: was the breakout tradable or did it fade?
- 10-day: did the move continue or fully fail?
- 20-day: did the breakout mature into a sustained trend or mean-revert back into the base?

## Analysis Template

- Distance from local lows: not the main trigger, but price should still be controllable.
- Fundamental improvement: preferred, not ignored.
- Volume confirmation: mandatory.
- Sector wake-up: strongly helpful.
- Clear support: breakout zone must become support.
- Dilution / structural risk: should remain low.
