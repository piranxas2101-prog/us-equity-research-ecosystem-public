# support_bounce_swing

## Philosophy

- Catch a short-term rebound near visible support before a broader upside swing becomes obvious.
- Best used when price is not extended, support is nearby, and risk can be invalidated clearly.

## Setup Rules

- Price is near the lower part of its recent range.
- Price interacts with support and starts to bounce rather than break cleanly below it.
- RSI is weak-to-constructive, not already overheated.
- MACD is positive or close to turning.
- Volume is at least acceptable for a developing bounce.
- Fresh filing / balance-sheet quality improves confidence.
- Common-stock structure is preferred.

## Disqualifiers

- Already extended price action.
- High-risk structure or obvious dilution flags.
- Price too far from local support.
- Very weak volume and no sign of reversal.

## Outcome Tracking

- 1-day: did the bounce hold?
- 3-day: did price gain distance from support without failing?
- 5-day: did the trade produce a tradable swing?
- 10-day: did the bounce turn into a larger move or fail back into the base?
- 20-day: did the setup develop into a full swing trend or fade after the initial reaction?

## Analysis Template

- Distance from local lows: should remain tight.
- Fundamental improvement: preferred, especially net cash / cash flow support.
- Volume confirmation: should be stable or improving.
- Sector wake-up: helpful but not mandatory.
- Clear support: mandatory.
- Dilution / structural risk: should be low.
