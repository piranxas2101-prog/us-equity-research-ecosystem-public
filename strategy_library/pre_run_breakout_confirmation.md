# pre_run_breakout_confirmation

## Philosophy

- Validate that a name nearing a breakout still has enough structure to be early rather than late.
- This is a bridge between accumulation and breakout logic.

## Setup Rules

- Price is pushing the upper part of the short-term range without being too stretched.
- The broader 60-day range still allows upside without obvious chase risk.
- RSI shows controlled strength.
- MACD supports continuation.
- Volume should be above average or improving.
- Risk remains manageable and the structure remains common-stock friendly.

## Disqualifiers

- Breakout is already too extended.
- RSI is too hot.
- Range position is too high for a first entry.
- Weak filing freshness or structural flags.

## Outcome Tracking

- 1-day: did the setup hold near the trigger zone?
- 3-day: did volume confirm the move?
- 5-day: did the breakout become a valid swing?
- 10-day: did the move continue or fail back into the prior range?
- 20-day: did the pre-run setup produce a sustainable trend after confirmation?

## Analysis Template

- Distance from local lows: less important than range control.
- Fundamental improvement: preferred.
- Volume confirmation: mandatory.
- Sector wake-up: strongly helpful.
- Clear support: breakout zone should become support.
- Dilution / structural risk: should stay low.
