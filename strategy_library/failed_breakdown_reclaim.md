# failed_breakdown_reclaim

## Philosophy

- Catch names that appear to lose support, fail to continue lower, and reclaim the prior range with improving momentum.
- Useful when panic or forced selling exhausts itself before a short-term reversal.

## Setup Rules

- Price tests or undercuts a support area and then reclaims it.
- Reclaim should happen with improving momentum or stabilization.
- RSI should move from weak into constructive territory.
- MACD should improve from negative toward neutral or positive.
- ATR and volatility must stay tradable rather than chaotic.
- Fundamentals should not be deteriorating sharply.

## Disqualifiers

- Breakdown continues and support is not reclaimed.
- Reclaim is weak and volume does not confirm.
- Structural risk dominates the setup.
- Price becomes extended immediately after the reclaim.

## Outcome Tracking

- 1-day: did the reclaim hold?
- 3-day: did reversal momentum build?
- 5-day: did the reclaim become a swing move?
- 10-day: did the move continue or roll back below support?
- 20-day: did the reclaim lead to a full trend repair or only a short-lived bounce?

## Analysis Template

- Distance from local lows: extremely relevant.
- Fundamental improvement: strongly preferred.
- Volume confirmation: important.
- Sector wake-up: helpful.
- Clear support: mandatory.
- Dilution / structural risk: must stay manageable.
